Where Is a Lap Won?

About this project →

A corner-by-corner look at 2025 Formula 1 qualifying. Every team's fastest lap at all 24 races, split into 403 corners and grouped into slow, medium and fast types.

Data: FastF1 (F1 live timing), 2025 qualifying sessions. Corner types: K-means (k = 3) implemented in NumPy. Built with D3.js. Source code: GitHub.
